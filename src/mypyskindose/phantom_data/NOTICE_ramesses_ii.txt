Modifications:
  The redistributed STL files are DERIVATIVES of the source scan. They were
  reoriented into the MyPySkinDose PSD (head-first supine) frame, uniformly
  scaled (body axis to ~185 cm; final height ~192 cm), remeshed to a single
  watertight manifold via solid voxelization + marching cubes (the raw scan was
  not watertight), and decimated (full mesh ~5.6k faces; reduced preview ~1k
  faces). See scripts/phantom_gen/fun_mesh_manifest.json and
  dev-docs/references/fun_phantom_provenance.md for the exact locked transform
  and repair details.
