Modifications:
  The redistributed STL files are DERIVATIVES of the source mesh. They were
  reoriented into the MyPySkinDose PSD (head-first supine) frame, uniformly
  scaled (height axis to ~120 cm; final height ~121.5 cm), remeshed to a single
  watertight manifold via solid voxelization + marching cubes (the raw scan was
  not watertight), Y-flipped for face-up supine, and kept at ~5.7k faces (full)
  with a ~1k-face reduced preview. See scripts/phantom_gen/fun_mesh_manifest.json
  and dev-docs/references/fun_phantom_provenance.md.
